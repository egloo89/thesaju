## Margin scenario, not a profit guarantee

At 6,900 KRW assume VAT reserve of price/11, PG+refund reserve 6% of gross, AI/compute ceiling 1,500 KRW per report and support/operations reserve 1,200 KRW per sale. Indicative contribution is about 3,158 KRW before customer acquisition and fixed costs. These are conservative planning assumptions, not measured costs or provider quotations. Current generation still computes all interpretation topics; verify actual usage before live payments. Implement per-order token budgets, durable entitlements and anti-abuse/rate limiting before enabling live keys. Unlimited lifetime plans can incur unbounded costs and cannot be guaranteed profitable; live charging remains disabled.
